# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Standards

**Expected behavior:**
- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Accept feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct.

Instances of abusive behavior can be reported to [hello@aipaygen.com](mailto:hello@aipaygen.com). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.

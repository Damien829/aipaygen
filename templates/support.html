Report a Bug

Found something broken? Please email support@aipaygen.com with:

  • The endpoint you called (e.g. /research)
  • The request_id from the error response
  • Your request body (redact sensitive data)
  • The error message you received

Including the request_id helps us find your request in our logs and resolve issues faster.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 22, 2026

AiPayGen ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website aipaygen.com and provides access to 65+ AI tools via our API and web application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

By accessing or using AiPayGen, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our services.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Email address — provided optionally when creating an account or subscribing to updates.
  • Payment information — processed securely through Stripe. We do not store your full credit card number, expiration date, or CVV on our servers.
  • API keys — generated when you register for API access.
  • Support requests — any content you submit through our support or contact forms.

Information collected automatically

  • IP address — collected with each request for security, rate limiting, and abuse prevention.
  • Usage data — which tools you use, request timestamps, response times, error rates, and general usage patterns.
  • Device and browser information — browser type, operating system, screen resolution, and referring URL.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — used for session management, preferences, analytics, and advertising (see Section 3).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our AI tools and API services.
  • Process transactions and manage billing through Stripe.
  • Monitor and enforce usage limits and rate limiting.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Improve and optimize our platform, tools, and user experience.
  • Send service-related communications (outage notices, billing confirmations, security alerts).
  • Display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use the content of your API requests to train AI models.

3. Cookies and Tracking

AiPayGen uses cookies and similar tracking technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies — required for session management, authentication, and core platform functionality.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Advertising cookies — used by Google AdSense to serve personalized ads based on your browsing behavior across websites.

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may prevent certain features from functioning correctly.

Google's use of advertising cookies is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third-party providers to operate our platform. Each provider has its own privacy policy governing their handling of your data:

Stripe

We use Stripe to process payments. When you make a purchase, your payment details are transmitted directly to Stripe's secure servers. We receive only a transaction token, last four digits of your card, and billing confirmation. Stripe is PCI DSS Level 1 certified.

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our site. Google may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your visits to AiPayGen and other websites. You can opt out of personalized ads through your Google account settings.

Cloudflare

We use Cloudflare for content delivery (CDN), DDoS protection, and security. Cloudflare may process your IP address and request metadata as part of its security and performance services.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data — retained for as long as your account is active, plus 30 days after deletion to allow recovery.
  • API usage logs — retained for up to 90 days for monitoring and debugging purposes, then aggregated or deleted.
  • Payment records — retained as required by applicable tax and financial regulations (typically 7 years).
  • IP addresses and access logs — retained for up to 90 days for security analysis.
  • API request/response content — not stored beyond the duration of the request unless explicitly enabled by you (e.g., conversation history features).

6. Your Rights

For all users

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — request that we update or correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Data portability — request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

European Economic Area (GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation, including:

  • The right to restrict processing of your personal data.
  • The right to object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Our legal bases for processing include: performance of a contract (providing the service), legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, platform improvement), consent (marketing, ad personalization), and legal obligation (financial records).

California (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act grants you:

  • The right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used.
  • The right to delete personal information we have collected.
  • The right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your CCPA rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@aipaygen.com. We will respond within 30 days (or within the applicable legal timeframe).

7. Children's Privacy

AiPayGen is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at support@aipaygen.com and we will promptly delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Effective date" at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you via email or a prominent notice on our website. Your continued use of AiPayGen after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

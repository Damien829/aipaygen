# Market notes (researched 2026-11-28)

Directional only. Several sources are marketing content and disagree with each other.

## Genres
- Sources conflict on the top earner. Some say simulators (pet collecting, progression) are still the most profitable, but also the most crowded. Others say RPGs, and one 2026 ranking says the biggest earners lean on cosmetic spending (Rivals, Dress to Impress, Brookhaven).
- Tycoons earn mostly through passes for faster progression, exclusive zones and aesthetics.
- Takeaway: Crystal Pets (pet sim plus tycoon-style zones) is a reasonable pick. Differentiate with a theme and strong retention, and add a cosmetics layer later.

## Payouts
- Roblox announced (April 2026) a 42% higher DevEx rate for Robux earned from verified US 18+ players, effective June 8, 2026. It applies to passes, subscriptions, select in-game items and private servers. Reported rates: about $0.0038 per Robux standard, about $0.0054 per Robux for 18+.
- Payout rates were also raised 8.5% in September 2025.
- Roblox paired this with discovery changes for "novel" games ("Standout Games" home section). Originality is rewarded, so the theme and hooks matter.
- Takeaway: games that appeal to older players earn more per Robux. Consider adult-friendly content (deeper progression, trading and collection) after launch.
